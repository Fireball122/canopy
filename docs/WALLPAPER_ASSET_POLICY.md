# Wallpaper Asset Policy

The `Wallpapers` folder may contain local sample artwork. Before a public release, maintain a source and license record for every image that ships with Canopy.

For each asset, record its filename, creator or source URL, license, attribution text, and any required distribution restrictions. Remove assets whose rights cannot be verified. Do not describe bundled wallpapers as project-owned or freely reusable unless their license supports that statement.

User-selected wallpapers are local files chosen by the user. Canopy does not upload them.
