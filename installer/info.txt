Welcome to Canopy — Zero-Notch Top Hover Desktop Control Center!

Canopy brings the fluid desktop overlay experience of Caelestia Shell (Wayland/Hyprland) to Windows 11 and Windows 10, combined with a high-performance visual wallpaper switcher.

==================================================================
KEY FEATURES INCLUDED:
==================================================================

* ZERO-NOTCH IDLE FOOTPRINT:
  Occupies 0 visible pixels when closed. Completely click-through
  so browser tabs, games, and desktop windows remain 100% interactive.

* TOP-EDGE HOVER SUMMON:
  Move your mouse cursor to the top center edge of the screen and
  dwell for ~120ms to smoothly slide down the floating frosted glass panel.

* 0% CPU DEEP SLEEP:
  WebView2 renderer, audio capture, and telemetry timers are automatically
  suspended when the panel slides off-screen, saving laptop battery.

* 40-BAND WASAPI AUDIO VISUALIZER & SYNCED LYRICS:
  Real-time audio spectrum analyzer and time-synchronized lyrics
  powered by the LRCLIB database with automatic album art lookup.

* PER-APPLICATION AUDIO MIXER:
  Adjust master volume, microphone level, or individual app volumes
  (Spotify, Discord, Chrome, games) directly from the Media view.

* VISUAL WALLPAPER CAROUSEL:
  Browse and apply desktop wallpapers instantly from %USERPROFILE%\Pictures\Wallpapers
  with automatic dominant color extraction and adaptive glass theming.

* GAME & PRESENTATION IMMUNITY:
  Automatically suppresses hover activation over full-screen DirectX/Vulkan games
  and active presentation screens.

==================================================================
DEFAULT SHORTCUTS:
==================================================================
  Toggle Overlay:   Win + ` (or configurable in Settings)
  Exit Panel:       Click outside or move cursor away (200ms debounce)
  Settings & Tray:  Right-click the Canopy icon in the taskbar tray
